CORE PHILOSOPHY

The Zomato Principle

Zomato does not remind you to eat. You already know how to eat. Zomato creates a moment of desire at the exact time you are vulnerable to it.

THE CONVENTIONAL GAP

The Student Who Gave Up

In a tier-2 college in India, a student opened the physics textbook and felt dread. They will never ask a robotic assistant for help because asking feels like admitting defeat.

Sending them an alert saying "Time to study Chapter 3: Electric Current" guarantees they swipe it away. The source feels like school.

THE PINCH ENGINE

Naming The Curiosity

We reach that same student on a Wednesday evening via WhatsApp with an invisible thread:

"Did you know PUBG was made entirely on C++? The same logic that moves your character is what moves iPhone factory robots in China right now."

No pressure to respond. But over weeks, the student who decided they weren't a science person starts asking questions.

BELIEF 01

Curiosity is engineered

Every student is curious about something. The system traces a thread from that passion into the syllabus.

BELIEF 02

Dependency is curriculum

Physics is not chapters. It is a single continuous thread of logic: Charge → Potential → Current → Magnetic Field.

BELIEF 03

Teacher is human bridge

Knowledge exists. The teacher's irreplaceable role is to make it land in another mind. We make them superhuman.

BELIEF 04

Notes are not learning

The question appears first. Notes only appear when requested. Passive copying is replaced by active recall.
